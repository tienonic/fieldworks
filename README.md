# FieldWorks

FieldWorks is about getting better information out of farm fields without making field work harder.

We are building field stations for soil, water, and weather data. The hardware is only part of it. A sensor reading needs the field event, location, maintenance history, and notes around it before it becomes useful.

## What we are working on

- One complete station that works outside, not just on a bench
- Sensor records tied to irrigation, maintenance, and field notes
- Hardware that can be repaired without rebuilding the whole system
- Data that is clear enough to review and export without guesswork

## Right now

The immediate job is to lock the first station design, test the full signal path, and run it in the field long enough to find the real problems. We will publish the design and results once they hold up.
