# SM-01: Soil monitoring station 1

Status: blocked
Owner: unassigned
Updated: 2026-07-27
Evidence: proposal architecture; compatibility grid; OPP request `MOGA-DENCDSO`

## Purpose

Measure soil water tension at three depths and soil temperature at one approved field location.

## At a glance

| Physical package | Data product | Field question |
|---|---|---|
| three 200SS probes + one 200TS + VA3 + ENTS box | shallow/middle/deep kPa, soil °C, health | Where is the profile drying, how quickly, and at what temperature? |

## Data expected from SM-01

| Field | Unit | Source | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tension_shallow_kpa` | kPa | shallow 200SS | water tension at approved shallow depth |
| `tension_middle_kpa` | kPa | middle 200SS | water tension at approved middle depth |
| `tension_deep_kpa` | kPa | deep 200SS | water tension at approved deep depth |
| `soil_temp_c` | °C | 200TS | soil temperature at the station |
| four `*_raw_v` fields | volt | VA3 outputs | diagnostic values before conversion |
| health fields | mixed | node and gateway | timestamp, firmware, battery when supported, RSSI, SNR, quality flags |

## Connections

| Source | Adapter/output | ENTS input | Firmware/data |
|---|---|---|---|
| 200SS shallow | VA3 channel, 0-3 V | ADC channel 1 | soil tension in centibars/kPa |
| 200SS middle | VA3 channel, 0-3 V | ADC channel 2 | soil tension in centibars/kPa |
| 200SS deep | VA3 channel, 0-3 V | ADC channel 3 | soil tension in centibars/kPa |
| 200TS | VA3 temperature channel, 0-3 V | ADC channel 4 | soil temperature in °C |
| solar and LiPo | BQ24210 charger and JST-PH | power subsystem | battery state |
| radio | Wio-E5 | US915 LoRaWAN | station ID `SM-01` |

The fourth ADC channel has an open pin-map conflict with another peripheral or the battery monitor. Resolve and lock the ENTS pin map before assembly.

## Bench acceptance

- Verify all four channels independently.
- Compare tension readings against a known dry/wet response and Irrometer conversion data.
- Compare temperature against a reference thermometer.
- Confirm depth labels survive installation and map to stable payload fields.

## Installation gates

Student Farm staff set the exact depths, horizontal spacing, crop zone, cable protection, and coordinates. The proposal's 6, 12, and 24 inch depths are provisional.
