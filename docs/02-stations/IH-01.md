## Purpose

Measure irrigation volume and line pressure, and provide a tested hardware path for valve control at one nominal 3/4-inch irrigation head.

## At a glance

| Physical package | Data product | Field question |
|---|---|---|
| D10 meter + SEN0257 + valve path + ENTS box | gallons, GPM, psi/MPa, valve command, health | How much water passed, at what pressure, and what did the controller ask the valve to do? |

## Data expected from IH-01

| Field | Unit | Source | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `volume_total_gal` | gallon | D10 pulses | cumulative delivered volume for the current counter epoch |
| `flow_rate_gpm` | gallon/minute | pulse delta/time | recent flow rate |
| `pressure_psi`, `pressure_mpa` | psi, MPa | SEN0257 | calibrated line pressure |
| `pressure_raw_v` | volt | divided ADC | diagnostic pressure signal |
| `valve_command` | enum | controller | last requested state; no position sensor installed |
| `valve_pulse_ms` | millisecond | controller | commanded latching-pulse duration |
| health fields | mixed | node and gateway | timestamp, firmware, battery when supported, RSSI, SNR, quality flags |

## Connections

| Source | Electrical path | ENTS input/output | Firmware/data |
|---|---|---|---|
| D10-C-SRS | two-wire dry contact; one lead GPIO, one ground; pull-up enabled | GPIO interrupt | count pulses; 1 pulse/gallon |
| SEN0257 | 5 V supply; 0.5-4.5 V signal through 22 kΩ/47 kΩ divider | ADC, 0.34-3.07 V | invert divider and convert to MPa/psi |
| valve solenoid | 3.7 V battery -> 9 V boost -> reversing momentary driver | GPIO command to driver | open/close pulse and reported state |
| solar panel | center-positive barrel input, below 18 V maximum | BQ24210 charger | battery telemetry if enabled |
| LiPo | JST-PH, polarity verified, onboard protection | ENTS battery input | node power |
| radio | Wio-E5 | US915 LoRaWAN | station ID `IH-01` |

## Bench acceptance

- Inventory each physical SKU and photograph labels. Store private packing records outside Git.
- Confirm D10 pipe size and pulse rate.
- Confirm pressure output stays below 3.3 V after the divider across the test range.
- Confirm the valve driver can latch and unlatch without holding current.
- Confirm battery polarity before connection.
- Produce decoded flow, pressure, valve command, battery, and timestamp fields.
