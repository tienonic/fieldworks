# Student Farm priorities, November 20, 2026

Status: working
Owner: Nicholas Melnichenko
Updated: 2026-11-20
Evidence: November 20 meeting with the Market Garden team and walk with the Student Farm director

The farm wants a few stations that answer practical questions, data from the water equipment it already owns in one place, and an interface people will actually check.

## Questions the farm wants answered

- **Soil temperature for planting:** when to cover crops on cold nights, when greenhouse starts can go to the field, whether to direct seed or transplant, and how this year compares with past years.
- **Farm-wide water:** the main meter is read about once a month by laptop and the files go unused. Bring it into the dashboard with a simple monthly upload first, then automatically.
- **Greenhouse water:** the greenhouse supply is not on the main meter; a flow sensor on its inlet would capture it. The greenhouse HOBO logger should join the same interface.
- **Wash station:** a new wash station is planned. Measure what share of the farm's water goes to washing compared with irrigation.
- **Water in versus soil moisture:** link the water applied to a bed with the moisture it produces, using the farm's in-row sensors where possible.
- **Drainage:** how long beds stay wet after storms and where water sits.

## What this changes in the plan

- Start with a weather station and a few soil stations, and ask for interface feedback early.
- Use farm-owned equipment first: the main meter, two spare 3/4-inch iPERL meters and DIG LEIT-1 ILV controllers ([physical inventory](../04-procurement/physical-inventory.md)).
- Add local logging on each board so readings survive radio gaps.

Some lines are 3/4 inch; confirm sizes with the farm team before choosing flow meters.
